The school behind your first wave

We started Wave Riders to share what the ocean has given us — and to prove that anyone, at any age, can learn to surf safely.

Our Story

From local lineup to surf school

Wave Riders began in 2010 with a single longboard, a clipboard, and a stretch of Bali shoreline. What started as weekend lessons for friends grew into a full school with three locations and an instructor team that knows every reef and current along the coast.

We're proud that most of our coaches grew up surfing these breaks. When you book with us, you're not just learning a sport — you're being welcomed into a community that has surfed, protected, and shared this coastline for generations.

Our mission is simple: get you confidently riding waves, faster, safer, and with more fun than you thought possible.

Safety and Trust

We take the ocean seriously so you don't have to worry

The ocean is powerful and we respect it. Every precaution is taken so your only job is to have fun.

  • ✓Trained water lifeguard present at every session
  • ✓Beginner sessions held in supervised, beginner-friendly breaks
  • ✓Rip current awareness taught before entering water
  • ✓Soft foam boards used for all beginner and kids lessons
  • ✓Lessons rescheduled if conditions are unsafe
🌊

Ocean Reading

We brief you on wave selection, rip currents, and safe entry/exit before every session.

🏥

First Aid Ready

All instructors hold current first aid certification. A kit is always on the beach.

☀️

Sun and Heat Management

We time sessions to avoid peak sun, provide rash guards, and remind you to hydrate.

📡

Daily Surf Check

Instructors review forecasts each morning to select the safest break for the day.
